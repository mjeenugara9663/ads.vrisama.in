Privacy, clearly explained.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Vrisama Ads is a creative advertising and automation initiative based in Bengaluru, India. This page explains how this demonstration website works. The website may be updated before public launch.

What is collected on this site?

The public pages do not contain a contact form and do not send enquiries to a Vrisama database. When you click WhatsApp or email, your device opens that third-party service. Information you choose to share is then handled by that service and by the recipient you contact.

Campaign Studio drafts

Campaign plans you enter in the Social Ad Planner are saved using your browser's localStorage on that device. They are not uploaded to Vrisama's servers or published to advertising networks by this website. You can clear drafts in the planner, clear your browser storage, or export drafts as CSV. Other people with access to your device and browser profile may be able to view locally saved drafts. Avoid entering confidential campaign details on shared devices.

Fonts and external links

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. External links may open WhatsApp or an email app. These providers have their own privacy practices.

Analytics

No visitor analytics service is connected in this version. The site includes local event hooks for future analytics development but does not transmit those events to a third-party analytics service. Any future tracking should be reviewed against applicable requirements before activation.
