FAQ

Good questions.

Do you manage the campaigns, or just advise?

Either. We can fully manage setup, creatives, monitoring and weekly reports, or support your team with planning and creatives. Live activation depends on your approved ad accounts, permissions, budget and agreed scope.

Which platforms do you run ads on?

Facebook, Instagram, Google Search, YouTube and LinkedIn. We suggest the mix that suits your audience and budget, rather than spreading you thin.

What does the AI actually do?

It replies to new enquiries with on-brand messages, logs each lead to your sheet, scores how ready they are, and alerts you when one looks hot. You stay in charge of pricing, promises and closing.

Can alerts come on WhatsApp or Telegram?

Yes, on whichever you prefer. WhatsApp automation depends on your WhatsApp Business setup and permissions, which we scope together.

Who owns the ad accounts and leads?

You do. Work is planned around access you approve, and your accounts and customer data stay under your control.

Do you guarantee a number of enquiries?

No. Results depend on audience, offer, competition, spend and follow-up. We focus on measurable activity and steady improvement.
