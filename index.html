07 / COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

We want you to know what you're getting before you commit.

Can Vrisama run ads on Facebook and Instagram?+

We can discuss campaign planning, creative preparation and setup support. Live ad activation depends on your approved Meta account, permissions, budget and agreed scope.

Does the Social Ad Scheduler publish ads automatically?+

No. It helps you plan and export drafts in your browser. Publishing paid ads requires a separate connection to each platform's advertising tools.

Who owns the ad accounts and leads?+

You do. We plan the work around access you approve, and client-owned accounts and customer data should stay under client control.

What budget or timeline should I expect?+

That depends on the platforms, number of creatives, landing page needs and follow-up setup. Contact us with your goal and approximate budget for a scoped discussion.

Do you guarantee a number of enquiries?+

No. Campaign results depend on the audience, offer, competition, spending and follow-up. We focus on measurable activity and iterative improvement.

How do I get started?+

Share your business, what you're promoting and an approximate monthly ad budget by WhatsApp or email. We'll discuss what an appropriate first project could include.
