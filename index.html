FAQ

Questions, answered

What does the AI actually do?

It helps draft ad copy and creative ideas, replies to new enquiries with on-brand messages, logs each lead to your sheet, scores how ready they are, and alerts you when a lead looks hot. You stay in charge of pricing, promises and closing.

Can alerts come on WhatsApp or Telegram?

Yes. We set up alerts on the channel you prefer. WhatsApp automation depends on your WhatsApp Business setup and permissions, which we scope together.

Can Vrisama run ads on Facebook and Instagram?

We can plan campaigns, prepare creative and support setup. Live activation depends on your approved Meta account, permissions, budget and agreed scope.

Who owns the ad accounts and leads?

You do. Work is planned around access you approve, and your accounts and customer data stay under your control.

What budget or timeline should I expect?

It depends on the platforms, number of creatives, landing page needs and automation setup. Share your goal and approximate budget and we'll scope it with you.

Do you guarantee a number of enquiries?

No. Results depend on audience, offer, competition, spend and follow-up. We focus on measurable activity and steady improvement.
